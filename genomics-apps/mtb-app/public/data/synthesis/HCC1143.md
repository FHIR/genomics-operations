## Reason for visit

First distant recurrence of triple-negative breast cancer (lung and mediastinal nodes, August 2026), confirmed on lung biopsy (2026-09-09) as still triple-negative with PD-L1 CPS 12. She has had no treatment for metastatic disease.

- What first-line treatment is supported for PD-L1-positive (CPS ≥ 10) metastatic TNBC, about 20 months after curative treatment ended?
- Do the tumor's genomic findings open any targeted options or trials, now or later?

The report lists only the two point mutations. The genomic data add structural changes from whole-genome sequencing, most notably the PTEN and chromosome 13 deletions. The report states no pathogenic PTEN or BRCA2 alterations; it may not have assessed structural variants, so this isn't necessarily a disagreement.

The Genomics Explorer also shows structural variants under PALB2, NTRK1, PIK3CA and other genes. These are large somatic inversions that span the genes without breaking them (for PALB2, a 13-Mb inversion on chromosome 16 whose nearest breakpoint is about 5 Mb away), so they aren't counted as findings. They don't contradict the negative germline PALB2 result, which concerns inherited variants. None of the findings carries FDA-labeled or CIViC level A/B therapeutic evidence in breast cancer.

## Cancer drivers

**Bottom line: this cancer is driven mostly by lost brakes, which no approved drug restores; its one accelerator, FGFR2, is targetable only in trials.**

| Pathway | Gene | Role | Change | Treatment status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| p53 (DNA-damage checkpoint) | TP53 | Brake | p.Arg248Gln | Watch: no approved TP53-directed therapy |
| PI3K growth pathway | PTEN | Brake | Part of the gene deleted | Watch: PTEN-directed drugs are approved only for HR-positive breast cancer |
| Growth-factor signaling | FGFR2 | Accelerator | p.Arg203Cys | Trial only: FGFR inhibitor trials open to any solid tumor (see Treatment options) |
| PI3K growth pathway | MAGI2 | Brake (minor) | Two partial deletions | Watch |
| Gene regulation | HDAC4 | Brake (minor) | Splice-site change | Watch |

Two brakes are off: p53, the cell's main checkpoint after DNA damage, and PTEN, which normally holds back the PI3K growth pathway. One accelerator is on: FGFR2, a growth-factor receptor switched on by a known activating change. Together with a heavily rearranged genome, this fits an aggressive triple-negative cancer that recurred early after chemotherapy. A large chromosome 13 deletion also removes at least one copy of BRCA2 and RB1; it isn't counted as a driver, but it's worth confirming whether both copies are lost, which would raise PARP inhibitors or platinum (BRCA2) and bear on CDK4/6 inhibitors (RB1). The data can't show which changes are present in every tumor cell. The Genomics Explorer's Pathways view shows these genes on the TP53, PI3K and RTK/RAS pathway diagrams.

## Information gaps

- **Copy number:** the structural data give no copy-number values. Copy number for the chromosome 13 deletion (BRCA2, RB1) and PTEN: whether both copies are lost would change the reading of BRCA2 (PARP inhibitor or platinum sensitivity) and RB1. PTEN loss by IHC would also help.
- **Genomic sample:** sequencing was on the 2024 primary. The 2026 lung metastasis hasn't been profiled.
- **Gene fusions:** the structural-variant test doesn't detect translocations, so fusions between chromosomes (e.g. NTRK, RET, FGFR2 fusions, which have tissue-agnostic or trial options) haven't been tested. RNA-based fusion testing would be needed.
- **Tumor mutational burden** isn't reported (TMB-high is a tissue-agnostic pembrolizumab indication, though pembrolizumab is already first-line here).
- **Data quality:** the genomic data's test dates (2020-02) don't match the report (2024-06-14), and the structural data include Y-chromosome variants in a female patient. The link between the server's data and the reported specimen should be confirmed.
- **Variant allele frequencies** are a fixed placeholder, so clonality can't be assessed.

## Treatment options

**First line for PD-L1-positive (CPS ≥ 10) metastatic TNBC** (her CPS is 12; recurrence about 20 months after curative treatment):

1. **Sacituzumab govitecan + pembrolizumab** (rank 1). Labeled for first-line unresectable locally advanced or metastatic TNBC with PD-L1 CPS ≥ 10. Approved June 2026 on the phase 3 ASCENT-04/KEYNOTE-D19 trial, which improved progression-free survival over pembrolizumab plus chemotherapy. *Why first:* the newest labeled option for exactly her setting, with a randomized advantage over the alternative. *Considerations:* ECOG 1 and normal organ function; the sacituzumab label carries a boxed warning for neutropenia and diarrhea (her blood counts are normal); hypothyroidism needs monitoring on pembrolizumab.
2. **Pembrolizumab + chemotherapy** (paclitaxel, nab-paclitaxel, or gemcitabine and carboplatin) (rank 2). Labeled for locally recurrent unresectable or metastatic TNBC with PD-L1 CPS ≥ 10. *Why second:* labeled and long established, but compared directly with option 1 and inferior in progression-free survival. *Considerations:* she had paclitaxel in 2024, more than 12 months ago; gemcitabine and carboplatin avoids repeating a taxane.

**Clinical trial to consider alongside option 1:** TROPION-Breast05 (NCT06103864) compares datopotamab deruxtecan, with or without durvalumab, against chemotherapy plus pembrolizumab in first-line PD-L1-positive (CPS ≥ 10) metastatic TNBC; she appears to meet the main criteria (see table).

**Later lines** (after first-line progression):

1. **Sacituzumab govitecan** alone, if not used first line: labeled after two or more prior systemic therapies, at least one for metastatic disease.
2. **FGFR inhibitor trials** for her FGFR2 hotspot (table below), which require prior standard treatment.

**Not indicated by her findings:**
- **PARP inhibitors (olaparib, talazoparib):** labeled for germline BRCA-mutated breast cancer. Her germline test was negative; the somatic chromosome 13 deletion including BRCA2 isn't a labeled indication.
- **Capivasertib:** its PTEN/PIK3CA/AKT1 indication in breast cancer is for HR-positive disease with fulvestrant. She is HR-negative.
- **Pemigatinib, erdafitinib:** labeled for FGFR2 fusions in cholangiocarcinoma and FGFR3 alterations in urothelial cancer. The report's pemigatinib implication for FGFR2 p.Arg203Cys is preclinical (level D).
- **Sunitinib:** the report and the TAPUR study show no benefit for FGFR alterations in breast cancer.
- **Trastuzumab deruxtecan:** labeled for HER2-positive, or HER2-low/ultralow with specific conditions (ultralow only in HR-positive disease). She is HER2 IHC 0 and HR-negative.
- **Datopotamab deruxtecan (labeled) and sacituzumab govitecan single agent first line:** labeled only for patients who aren't candidates for PD-1/PD-L1 therapy. She is.

## Emerging evidence

Searches covering 2025-07-10 to 2026-11-06 (PubMed and web, FDA announcements, ASCO/ESMO/AACR) found:

- **ASCENT-04/KEYNOTE-D19** (phase 3), published in *NEJM* (January 2026): sacituzumab govitecan plus pembrolizumab reduced the risk of progression or death by 35% versus pembrolizumab plus chemotherapy (median PFS 11.2 vs. 7.8 months) in first-line PD-L1-positive metastatic TNBC. This now underlies option 1's label (June 2026); overall survival data aren't yet reported in the sources found.
- **FGFR inhibitors in FGFR-altered breast cancer:** the RAGNAR basket study reported a 31% response rate (95% CI 11-59) to erdafitinib in 16 pretreated breast cancer patients with FGFR1-4 alterations, with responses in FGFR2/3 mutations (ASCO 2024; updated in *JCO Precision Oncology* 2026). Small numbers, and not specific to FGFR2 p.Arg203Cys; relevant to the FGFR trials above, not a basis for off-label use.

No new evidence was found for TP53 p.Arg248Gln-directed therapy (the only related trials are an early-phase vaccine study recruiting in China and a TP53 R248Q-specific T-cell therapy study not yet recruiting) or for PTEN loss in TNBC.
