# Case synthesis: Betty Breast (MRN HCC1143)

> **Demo: synthesis pre-generated for this case** (2026-10-22). Research demonstration, synthetic patient, not for clinical use.

## Case summary

- **Patient:** 54-year-old woman, ex-smoker; her mother had breast cancer at 64.
- **Diagnosis (April 2024):** triple-negative invasive ductal carcinoma of the right breast. Nottingham grade 3; ER 0%, PR 0%, HER2 IHC 0; Ki-67 70%.
- **Primary treatment:**
  - right partial mastectomy with sentinel node biopsy: pT2 (2.3 cm) pN0 (0/3), stage IIA
  - adjuvant dose-dense AC × 4 followed by weekly paclitaxel × 12, completed November 2024
  - whole-breast radiation, completed December 2024
- **Germline (inherited) multigene panel on blood:** no pathogenic variants, including in BRCA1, BRCA2 and PALB2.
- **Distant recurrence (August 2026, about 21 months after adjuvant chemotherapy):**
  - bilateral lung nodules and mediastinal lymph nodes; no liver, bone or brain involvement on PET-CT and brain MRI
  - lung biopsy: metastatic breast carcinoma, still ER 0%, PR 0%, HER2 IHC 0
  - PD-L1 (22C3) combined positive score **12**
  - CA 15-3 71 U/mL
- **Current status:**
  - ECOG 1
  - hemoglobin 11.6 g/dL (mildly low); kidney and liver function normal
  - hypothyroidism on levothyroxine (relevant to monitoring if an immune checkpoint inhibitor is used); no known drug allergies
  - she has had no systemic therapy since 2024

## Genomic findings (tumor sequencing)

These are acquired (somatic) changes in the tumor. They are separate from the germline panel above, which tested for inherited variants and was negative.

| Gene | Finding | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| TP53 | **R248Q**, somatic | ClinVar pathogenic or likely pathogenic. No therapeutic evidence in breast cancer (only a preclinical CIViC item) |
| BRCA2, RB1 | Large somatic deletion spanning both genes | No CIViC evidence |
| PTEN | Somatic deletion (about 12 kb) | No CIViC evidence |
| NTRK1, PALB2, PIK3CA | Large somatic inversions with breakpoints in these genes; functional impact unknown | No CIViC evidence. The PALB2 breakpoint is acquired in the tumor and doesn't contradict the negative germline PALB2 result |

None of the genomic findings carries therapeutic evidence for breast cancer.

## Information gaps

- **Structural variants:** copy number and functional impact of the BRCA2/RB1 and PTEN deletions aren't reported (e.g. PTEN loss by IHC).
- **NTRK1 inversion:** whether it produces an expressed fusion would need RNA-based testing.
- **Genomic sample:** sequencing was performed on the 2024 primary tumor. The 2026 metastasis hasn't been sequenced.

## Treatment options

**First line for metastatic TNBC with PD-L1 CPS ≥ 10** (her CPS is 12):
1. **Pembrolizumab + chemotherapy.** Labeled for locally recurrent unresectable or metastatic TNBC with PD-L1 CPS ≥ 10.
2. **Pembrolizumab + sacituzumab govitecan.** Labeled for first-line treatment of unresectable locally advanced or metastatic TNBC with PD-L1 CPS ≥ 10.

*Considerations for both:* she received an anthracycline and a taxane in the adjuvant setting, with recurrence about 21 months after completing them.

**Later lines:**

3. **Sacituzumab govitecan (single agent).** Labeled for metastatic TNBC after two or more prior systemic therapies, at least one for metastatic disease.

**Not indicated by her findings:**
- **Trastuzumab deruxtecan:** labeled for HER2-low disease (IHC 1+ or 2+/ISH−). The HER2-ultralow indication is limited to hormone receptor–positive disease. She is HER2 IHC 0 and triple-negative.
- **PARP inhibitors (olaparib, talazoparib):** labeled for germline BRCA-mutated disease. Her germline testing was negative. The somatic deletion spanning BRCA2 is not a labeled indication.
- **NTRK inhibitors:** labeled for NTRK gene fusions. An inversion involving NTRK1 isn't a confirmed fusion.
