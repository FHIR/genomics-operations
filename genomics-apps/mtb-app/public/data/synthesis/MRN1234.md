## Treatment options

1. **Add ribociclib to the aromatase inhibitor (adjuvant).** Labeled with an aromatase inhibitor for HR-positive, HER2-negative stage II–III early breast cancer at high risk of recurrence. The supporting trial (NATALEE) enrolled anatomic stage IIB–III, or node-negative stage IIA with grade 2 and high genomic risk or Ki-67 ≥ 20%.
   - She meets these criteria as recorded (stage IIB). She would also meet them if restaged as pT2 (stage IIA, grade 2, Oncotype 47).
   - *Considerations:* she is already on anastrozole (the trial used a non-steroidal aromatase inhibitor, with goserelin as indicated). Menopausal status should be confirmed.
2. **Continue adjuvant endocrine therapy** (anastrozole), alone or with ribociclib.
3. **Germline genetic counseling and testing for PALB2.** The 2024 ASCO–SSO guideline supports offering testing for high-penetrance genes beyond BRCA1/2, including PALB2, when family history is supportive, plus individualized counseling for pathogenic variants. Her family history includes breast, ovarian and pancreatic cancer. Germline confirmation would also inform testing for her relatives.

**Not indicated by her findings:**
- **Adjuvant olaparib:** labeled for germline BRCA-mutated HER2-negative high-risk early breast cancer. PALB2 is not included.
- **NTRK inhibitors:** labeled for NTRK gene fusions. Her NTRK3 finding is a single-nucleotide variant.

**Potential clinical trials:** no recruiting trial on ClinicalTrials.gov (as of 2026-10-22) matched her situation. Searches covered PALB2-related breast cancer studies and adjuvant therapy for high-risk HR-positive early breast cancer. The PALB2 studies found were for metastatic disease or required no personal history of breast cancer.
