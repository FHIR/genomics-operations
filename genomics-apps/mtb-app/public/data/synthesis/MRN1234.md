## Cancer drivers

**Bottom line: an inherited PALB2 defect plus acquired loss of four tumor brakes; none of these is targeted by a treatment for early breast cancer today, so endocrine therapy and the CDK4/6 question drive the decision.**

| p53 (DNA-damage checkpoint) | TP53 | Brake | p.Val173Leu | Watch |
| TGF-β growth control | SMAD4 | Brake | p.Asp351His | Watch |
| WNT signaling | RNF43 | Brake | Truncating | Watch |
| Chromatin remodeling | ARID1A | Brake | Truncating | Watch |

She inherited one broken copy of PALB2, a gene that works with BRCA2 to repair damaged DNA. In carriers, breast cancers often lose the second copy, which makes them depend on other repair routes, the basis for PARP inhibitor activity in advanced disease. Whether this tumor lost its second PALB2 copy can't be told from these data. The tumor also lost several brakes: p53 (the cell's main damage checkpoint), SMAD4 (a TGF-β growth-control signal), RNF43 (which normally restrains WNT growth signaling) and ARID1A (a chromatin regulator). This fits the high-risk biology suggested by her Oncotype score of 47. None of these is a treatment target in early breast cancer; they matter mainly if the cancer recurs, when PALB2 and the other changes could open trials. The data can't show which changes are present in every tumor cell. The Genomics Explorer's Pathways view shows TP53, SMAD4 and RNF43 on the TP53, TGF-β and WNT pathway diagrams.

## Treatment options

**Adjuvant therapy now** (HR-positive, HER2-negative, node-negative, 2.5 cm, grade 2, Oncotype 47; on anastrozole):

1. **Add ribociclib to anastrozole** (rank 1). Labeled with an aromatase inhibitor for HR-positive, HER2-negative stage II–III early breast cancer at high risk of recurrence. The pivotal trial (NATALEE) included node-negative tumors of 2–5 cm with grade 2 and high genomic risk, which she meets (Oncotype 47) whether her tumor is staged T2 or T3. *Why first:* labeled for her situation, with an invasive disease-free survival benefit. *Considerations:* the label requires ECG and electrolyte monitoring (QT prolongation), liver tests and blood counts, and warns of interstitial lung disease and severe skin reactions; the trial used goserelin where indicated, so menopausal status should be confirmed.
2. **Continue anastrozole alone** (rank 2). Standard adjuvant endocrine therapy. *Why second:* omits the added benefit of ribociclib she qualifies for.

**Co-occurring alterations:** CIViC co-occurring-variant evidence was checked for her alterations; none applies (the profiles that include TP53 or SMAD4 are in pancreatic cancer and require variants she doesn't have).

**For the inherited PALB2 variant:**
- **Genetic counseling**, and testing offered to relatives (her sister and other family members): the 2024 ASCO–Society of Surgical Oncology guideline on germline testing in breast cancer supports testing beyond BRCA1/2 and counseling for pathogenic variants. Her family history (breast, ovarian and pancreatic cancer) fits.
- **Smoking cessation** support (smokes daily; 20 pack-years).

**Not indicated by her findings:**
- **Adjuvant olaparib:** labeled for germline **BRCA**-mutated HER2-negative high-risk early breast cancer; PALB2 isn't included, and its trial (OlympiA) enrolled only BRCA1/2 carriers.
- **Abemaciclib:** its adjuvant label requires node-positive disease. She is node-negative.
- **NTRK inhibitors:** labeled for NTRK gene fusions. Her NTRK3 change is a splice-site variant, not a fusion.

## Emerging evidence

Searches covering 2025-07-10 to 2026-11-06 (PubMed and web, FDA announcements, ASCO/ESMO/AACR) found:

- **NATALEE 5-year analysis** (ESMO, October 2025): invasive disease-free survival 85.5% with ribociclib plus an aromatase inhibitor vs. 81.0% with the aromatase inhibitor alone; in node-negative patients the benefit was significant for the first time (HR 0.606, 95% CI 0.372–0.986). Distant disease-free survival benefit was seen across subgroups, including node-negative (2026 analysis). Overall survival data are immature. Supports option 1 for a node-negative patient like her.
- **PARP inhibitors after early PALB2-associated breast cancer:** no new evidence found. Adjuvant data remain limited to BRCA1/2 carriers.
