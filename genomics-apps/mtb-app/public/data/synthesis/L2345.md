# Case synthesis: Larry Lung (MRN L2345)

> **Demo: synthesis pre-generated for this case** (2026-10-22). Research demonstration, synthetic patient, not for clinical use.

## Case summary

- **Patient:** 69-year-old man, never smoker, with stage IVB (cT2b cN1 cM1c) adenocarcinoma of the right lower lobe, diagnosed May 2023. He had bone metastases (left ilium, T10, right 6th rib) and contralateral lung nodules.
- **Initial profiling:** EGFR L858R, with KRAS and BRAF wild type; PD-L1 TPS 5%.
- **First-line treatment:** osimertinib with carboplatin and pemetrexed from June 2023, then pemetrexed maintenance. He also received palliative radiation to the left ilium (20 Gy/5 fractions) and continues zoledronic acid.
- **Response:** partial response, followed by about 2.5 years of stable disease.
- **Progression on 2026-09-28:**
  - primary enlarged to 3.4 cm (nadir 2.1 cm)
  - new liver metastases (segments 6 and 7) and a new left lower lobe nodule
  - CEA rose from 4.0 to 21.7 ng/mL
  - brain MRI on 2026-10-02 showed no intracranial disease
- **Liver biopsy 2026-10-06:** metastatic adenocarcinoma, with no small cell or squamous transformation. Pemetrexed was stopped; osimertinib was continued pending this review.
- **Current status:**
  - ECOG 1
  - AST 58 and ALT 63 U/L (mildly elevated, in the setting of liver metastases); bilirubin normal
  - hemoglobin 12.1 g/dL; creatinine 1.1 (eGFR 72)
  - comorbid hypertension and hyperlipidemia; penicillin allergy (rash)

BRAF V600E and KRAS G12C were not detected at diagnosis (KRAS and BRAF were wild type in 2023) and are now present alongside persistent EGFR L858R and T790M. This pattern is consistent with acquired resistance after osimertinib, but the profile has no sample date, so the specimen for each variant can't be confirmed.

## Information gaps

- **MET:** copy number 5 is reported, but definitions of MET amplification vary by assay and trial. No MET FISH or IHC is recorded.
- **Variant allele frequencies** aren't available, so the relative size of the BRAF, KRAS and MET subclones is unknown.
- **PD-L1** hasn't been reported on the 2026 liver biopsy.

## Treatment options

**FDA-labeled options after progression on an EGFR TKI:**
1. **Amivantamab + carboplatin + pemetrexed.** Labeled for EGFR exon 19 deletion or L858R NSCLC that has progressed on or after an EGFR TKI. *Consideration:* he has already received carboplatin and pemetrexed, and progressed on pemetrexed maintenance. The label doesn't exclude prior platinum, but the same chemotherapy backbone would be repeated.
2. **Datopotamab deruxtecan.** Labeled for EGFR-mutated NSCLC after prior EGFR-directed therapy and platinum-based chemotherapy, both of which he has received.

**Single-agent options matched to the new genomic findings** (labeled for the alteration, but not in the setting of a co-existing EGFR driver):

3. **BRAF V600E: dabrafenib + trametinib.** Labeled for metastatic NSCLC with BRAF V600E.
4. **KRAS G12C: sotorasib or adagrasib.** Labeled as single agents for KRAS G12C NSCLC after at least one prior systemic therapy.
5. **MET copy gain:** no FDA-labeled MET-targeted therapy applies. Capmatinib and tepotinib are labeled only for MET exon 14 skipping, which isn't present.

*Consideration for options 3–5:* each alteration appeared alongside persistent EGFR L858R/T790M after osimertinib. Targeting the new alteration alone leaves the EGFR driver untreated.

**Combination therapy for co-occurring alterations.** Only combinations supported by an FDA label or by CIViC co-occurring-variant evidence (level A or B) are listed:
- **Amivantamab + carboplatin + pemetrexed** (option 1): FDA-labeled after progression on an EGFR TKI. Amivantamab is a bispecific EGFR- and MET-directed antibody.
- **No other combination is supported for this patient.** CIViC co-occurring-variant evidence matching his variants:
  - **EGFR L858R + T790M:** osimertinib sensitivity in NSCLC, **level A**. He has already received osimertinib and progressed on it.
  - **BRAF V600E + EGFR L858R + T790M:** osimertinib *resistance* in lung adenocarcinoma, level C (submitted, not yet reviewed). This is consistent with his progression, but it isn't a basis for a treatment recommendation.
- No FDA label or level A/B CIViC co-occurrence evidence supports targeted combinations for EGFR + MET, EGFR + BRAF, or EGFR + KRAS G12C here.
- An EGFR + MET combination (capmatinib + osimertinib) is available within a clinical trial (NCT05642572, below), if MET amplification is confirmed by the trial's definition.

**Not recommended:** continuing osimertinib alone. Its level A evidence (for T790M, and for L858R with T790M) predates the current progression on osimertinib. CIViC co-occurrence evidence links BRAF V600E with EGFR L858R/T790M to osimertinib resistance (level C).
