The tumor's main accelerator is EGFR. It is switched on by the exon 19 deletion, and the extra copies of EGFR add more of the same signal. This is what EGFR inhibitors target. Two brakes are damaged on one copy each, PRKDC (DNA repair) and PTPN13. The test detects copy-number changes, but no losses are recorded anywhere in the genome, so whether losses were assessed is unclear and whether the other copy is also lost can't be told. PTPN13's change is present in a smaller share of the reads (15%), so it may be in only part of the tumor, but tumor purity isn't recorded and clonality can't be determined. The 3-copy gains of KIT, PDGFRA and BCL9 are low-level and of little known significance. The TP53 change is pathogenic as an inherited variant, but no tumor classification or hotspot status counts it as a driver here. Pathway diagrams are in the app's Pathways view.

## Treatment options

The decision depends first on whether the disease has recurred. The options are given for each case. No combination for co-occurring alterations applies: every co-occurring-variant combination involving EGFR in CIViC needs a change she doesn't have (e.g., T790M, MET alterations).

### If there is no recurrence: adjuvant therapy

**1. Osimertinib, adjuvant, up to 3 years (recommended)**
- *Evidence:* Tagrisso label (2026-09-14) covers adjuvant therapy after tumor resection for NSCLC with an EGFR exon 19 deletion or L858R. CIViC EID11219, level A. In the trial (ADAURA), stage II–IIIA disease had a disease-free survival HR of 0.17 and an overall survival HR of 0.49 vs placebo.
- *Considerations:*
  - She meets the label: resected, exon 19 deletion, adjuvant chemotherapy completed, no prior EGFR inhibitor.
  - The trial enrolled within 26 weeks of surgery. She is about 64 weeks out, so benefit when starting this late is unproven.
  - Recurrence must be excluded first.
  - Mutation confirmation by an FDA-approved test is needed.
  - Before starting: cardiac assessment if she has cardiac risk factors, and a CBC. ECOG 1 and normal labs support treatment.
- *Rank reason:* FDA label for this setting, phase 3 overall survival benefit, and an oral drug suited to ECOG 1.

**2. Pembrolizumab, adjuvant, up to 1 year (not recommended over osimertinib)**
- *Evidence:* Keytruda and Keytruda Qlex labels (2026-10-16) cover adjuvant treatment after resection and platinum chemotherapy for stage IB (T2a ≥4 cm), II or IIIA NSCLC, with no EGFR exclusion. In KEYNOTE-091, the disease-free survival HR was 0.73 among patients who had adjuvant chemotherapy.
- *Considerations:*
  - Her PD-L1 is 0%.
  - CIViC EID7586 (level B): EGFR exon 19 deletions respond poorly to PD-1 inhibitors.
  - An indirect comparison (2023) favored adjuvant osimertinib over chemotherapy plus immunotherapy in EGFR-mutant resected NSCLC.
  - Timing: the label states no window for starting after surgery or chemotherapy, and the trial's registered eligibility criteria (NCT02504372) list none either; how starting about 12 months after chemotherapy compares with the trial population is unknown.
- *Rank reason:* Labeled, but the evidence specific to her tumor points away from it.

**Not indicated by her findings**
- Atezolizumab, adjuvant (Tecentriq label 2026-10-13): requires PD-L1 ≥1%; hers is 0%.
- Durvalumab or nivolumab perioperative regimens (Imfinzi 2026-08-31, Opdivo 2026-08-12): they start before surgery and require no known EGFR mutation (Opdivo's neoadjuvant-only indication also starts before surgery).
- Pembrolizumab perioperative (Keytruda label 2026-10-16): starts with chemotherapy before surgery; her tumor is already resected.
- Osimertinib after chemoradiation (Tagrisso): for unresectable stage III; her tumor was resected.
- Imatinib for the KIT/PDGFRA gains (Gleevec label 2026-07-13): no lung cancer indication. CIViC KIT amplification evidence is resistance in melanoma (EID1466).
- Tissue-agnostic therapies: her tumor is microsatellite stable, TMB 2.2 (below the label's TMB-high threshold of ≥13 mutations/Mb), and BRAF V600E negative. NTRK/RET fusions were not tested, and HER2 IHC was not done. HER2-mutant (Enhertu, Hernexeos) and EGFR exon 20 insertion (Zegfrovy) labels don't match her tumor.
- **Not recommended:** nivolumab + ipilimumab on the strength of the PRKDC change. CIViC EID13225 (level B) rests on 6 patients and doesn't account for her EGFR mutation. The Opdivo label's first-line ipilimumab indications require no EGFR or ALK aberrations.

### If recurrence is confirmed: first-line treatment for advanced disease

She hasn't been treated for advanced disease. Adjuvant carboplatin/paclitaxel ended 2025-11-30.

**1 (tied). Osimertinib + pemetrexed + platinum chemotherapy**
- *Evidence:* Tagrisso label (2026-09-14). CIViC EID12015, level A. In FLAURA2, overall survival was 47.5 vs 37.6 months vs osimertinib alone (HR 0.77).
- *Considerations:*
  - Grade ≥3 adverse events in 70% vs 34%.
  - She has had platinum adjuvantly, ending about 12 months ago.

**1 (tied). Amivantamab + lazertinib**
- *Evidence:* Rybrevant (IV, 2025-11-11) and Rybrevant Faspro (subcutaneous, 2026-11-05) labels, with the Lazcluze label (2026-05-05). CIViC EID12131, level A. In MARIPOSA, overall survival improved vs osimertinib (HR 0.75; 3-year survival 60% vs 51%).
- *Considerations:* Grade ≥3 adverse events in 80% vs 52% (skin, venous thromboembolism, infusion reactions).
- *Rank reason for the tie:* both regimens have phase 3 overall survival benefit over osimertinib alone, with more toxicity. There is no head-to-head trial, so the choice rests on her preferences and tolerance.

**3. Osimertinib alone**
- *Evidence:* Tagrisso label, first-line metastatic. In FLAURA, overall survival HR 0.80 vs gefitinib or erlotinib.
- *Rank reason:* less toxic, but shorter survival than the two combinations.

**4. Earlier-generation EGFR inhibitors:** erlotinib (erlotinib tablets label 2024-06-26; CIViC EID2995, level A), erlotinib + ramucirumab (Cyramza 2026-06-09; EID11240), afatinib (Gilotrif 2025-11-13; EID2996), dacomitinib (Vizimpro 2026-06-23; EID11239), gefitinib (Iressa 2023-02-28; EID11241).
- *Rank reason:* labeled first-line, but osimertinib improved survival over gefitinib/erlotinib. CIViC level B evidence links EGFR amplification to benefit from erlotinib or gefitinib (EID5925, EID948), but this is not comparative.

*Later lines, after EGFR therapy:* amivantamab + carboplatin + pemetrexed (Rybrevant label; EID12156) and datopotamab deruxtecan after EGFR therapy and platinum (Datroway 2026-05-27, accelerated approval; EID12522, EID12523). Pembrolizumab + chemotherapy after TKI failure is not supported (EID13097, level B, KEYNOTE-789).

## Emerging evidence

Searches covered 2023-02-28 to 2026-11-17 (PubMed, FDA approval announcements, 2026 meeting reports).

- **Adjuvant osimertinib, 8-year survival (ADAURA exploratory update):** *J Thorac Oncol*, published online 2026-09-13; presented at the IASLC 2026 World Conference on Lung Cancer (abstract PL03.01, per ASCO Post and OncLive reports, September 2026). Post hoc follow-up of a phase 3 trial. In stage II–IIIA disease the overall survival HR was 0.53, with 8-year survival 74% vs 58%. For exon 19 deletions the HR was 0.45. This supports the durability of option 1 but doesn't address starting about 15 months after surgery.
- **EGFR amplification with EGFR mutation (retrospective, 781 patients):** *Lung Cancer*, 2026. Concurrent amplification was linked to worse outcomes with first-line EGFR inhibitors only in L858R tumors, not significantly overall. She has an exon 19 deletion, so the finding doesn't change her options. It is hypothesis-generating.
- **Co-alterations and first-line osimertinib (real-world, 219 patients):** *Lung Cancer*, 2025. The number of co-alterations, co-alterations grouped by pathway, and TP53 alone did not predict outcome; TP53 + PIK3CA together did, and she has no PIK3CA change.
- No FDA approval ahead of the current labels was found for her setting (FDA Oncology/Hematologic Malignancies Approval Notifications, read 2026-11-17; latest entry 2026-11-16). The FLAURA2 and MARIPOSA overall survival results are already in the labels.
