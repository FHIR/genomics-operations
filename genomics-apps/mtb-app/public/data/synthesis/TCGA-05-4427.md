KRAS G12V locks the RAS growth switch in the "on" position, and the RAF1 hotspot sits one step further down the same pathway, so the main growth signal is switched on twice over. Loss of the TP53 brake removes a key check on damaged cells, a common partner of KRAS in smoking-related lung adenocarcinoma. The many smaller hits (TSC2, CREBBP, KDM6A, CDH1 and others) are single-copy or one-hit changes whose weight is uncertain; for TSC2, CREBBP and KDM6A a second hit on the other copy was not found, so it can't be said that the gene is fully lost. ## Treatment options

### Current decision: further treatment after resection and chemotherapy

**1 (tied). Adjuvant pembrolizumab (Keytruda, or subcutaneous Keytruda Qlex), up to 12 months.** Labeled "as a single agent… as adjuvant treatment following resection and platinum-based chemotherapy for adult patients with Stage IB (T2a ≥4 cm), II, or IIIA NSCLC" (Keytruda label 2026-10-16). In the supporting trial (KEYNOTE-091), among patients who had adjuvant chemotherapy, disease-free survival improved (hazard ratio 0.73); overall survival was immature.
- *Considerations:* her stage (pT2 pN1, IIB) and prior cisplatin fit the label, and no PD-L1 level is required. ECOG 0, with the blood counts and kidney and liver tests listed above. The label sets no deadline for starting, but she finished chemotherapy about 22 months ago; the trial's registered eligibility criteria give no interval, and no evidence was found on starting this long after surgery. The trial excluded patients with evidence of disease on baseline CT or brain imaging, more than 4 adjuvant cycles, autoimmune disease or pneumonitis, and any prior solid tumor unless in remission for at least 5 years (non-melanoma skin cancer excepted); her melanoma is undated, so the trial result may not apply to her, although the label itself has no such restriction.

**1 (tied). Adjuvant atezolizumab (Tecentriq, or subcutaneous Tecentriq Hybreza), up to 1 year.** Labeled "as adjuvant treatment following resection and platinum-based chemotherapy for adult patients with Stage II to IIIA NSCLC whose tumors have PD-L1 expression on ≥1% of tumor cells, as determined by an FDA-authorized test" (Tecentriq label 2026-10-13). In IMpower010, disease-free survival improved in stage II–IIIA with PD-L1 ≥1% (hazard ratio 0.66), and in the pre-specified subgroup with PD-L1 ≥50% (hazard ratio 0.43); overall survival was exploratory (hazard ratio 0.77).
- *Considerations:* her stage and PD-L1 82% fit, but the PD-L1 result is from the 22C3 assay and the label requires an FDA-authorized test (the trial used SP263). The label specifies starting after resection and up to 4 cycles of platinum-based chemotherapy. IMpower010 enrolled patients 4–12 weeks after complete resection, then gave cisplatin-based chemotherapy before randomization; she is far outside that window. It also excluded other malignancies within 5 years of randomization, so with her melanoma undated the trial result may not apply to her (the label has no such restriction).

*Why ranked together:* both are FDA-labeled for her stage after platinum chemotherapy, with disease-free survival benefit in phase 3 trials. Atezolizumab's data in PD-L1 ≥50% are stronger but hinge on the assay question; pembrolizumab needs no PD-L1 result. For both, the key uncertainty is the same: no evidence was found for benefit when adjuvant immunotherapy starts more than two years after surgery (her resection specimen is dated 2024-09-22) and about 22 months after chemotherapy; and her undated prior melanoma may have excluded her from both supporting trials. If the board judges that the adjuvant window has passed, the alternative is continued surveillance, with these drugs held for recurrence (below).

No combination therapy for co-occurring alterations applies: the co-occurring-variant evidence was checked for her alterations and none matches.

### If the cancer recurs (a later decision)

**1. First-line PD-1/PD-L1 inhibitor, alone or with chemotherapy.** For metastatic NSCLC with no EGFR or ALK aberrations, labels include pembrolizumab alone (PD-L1 TPS ≥1%) or with pemetrexed and platinum (nonsquamous), atezolizumab alone (PD-L1 ≥50% of tumor cells), and cemiplimab (Libtayo, label 2026-08-31) alone (TPS ≥50%) or with platinum chemotherapy, both requiring no EGFR, ALK or ROS1 aberrations.
- *Considerations:* PD-L1 82% meets every threshold; EGFR exon 19/L858R and ALK fusion were not found (fusions per the lab report). CIViC also links KRAS mutation with benefit from PD-1/PD-L1 inhibitors (EID4863, level B). If she receives adjuvant immunotherapy now, a recurrence during or soon after it would change this choice. Ranked first because these are labeled first-line options that fit her biomarkers; the choice among them and whether to add chemotherapy would depend on the recurrence.

**2. KRAS-directed clinical trials** (table below), after or alongside standard treatment, depending on each trial's prior-therapy rules.

**3. Sorafenib maintenance (CIViC EID12700, level B, submitted).** In a randomized-discontinuation phase 2 trial across advanced solid tumors with KRAS codon 12/13 changes (including G12V), KRAS amplification or RET M918T, patients with stable disease after 12 weeks of sorafenib who continued it had longer progression-free survival than those who stopped (median 5.6 vs 2.0 months).
- *Considerations:* evidence is mixed-tumor, small (35 randomized) and not lung-specific; ranked below labeled immunotherapy and trials for that reason, and relevant only for advanced disease after standard options.

The Keytruda TMB-high indication (TMB ≥13 mut/Mb; hers is 31.7) applies only to unresectable or metastatic tumors that have progressed after prior treatment with no satisfactory alternatives, so it would come after the options above, and would need confirmation that the TMB was measured with an FDA-authorized test.

## Emerging evidence

Searches covered 2026-02-17 to 2026-11-17 (PubMed, FDA approval announcements in trade press, and ClinicalTrials.gov). Nothing found is yet in a lung cancer label or in CIViC at level A or B for her disease.

- **Daraxonrasib in RAS-mutant lung cancer.** Phase 1/2 study, 136 patients with previously treated advanced RAS-mutant NSCLC: objective responses in 31–37% depending on dose; grade ≥3 adverse events in 54% (Arbour et al., *N Engl J Med* 2026, DOI 10.1056/NEJMoa2504059). Daraxonrasib was approved in the US on 2026-08-26 for metastatic pancreatic cancer only (Syed, *Drugs* 2026; Rasonque label 2026-08-27); CIViC's level A evidence for RAS G12 mutations is in pancreatic cancer (EID13411). *Bearing on this patient:* relevant only at recurrence; the phase 3 lung trial (NCT06881784) is recruiting.
- **KRAS G12V lung cancer and immunotherapy.** Retrospective study of 636 patients with KRAS G12V NSCLC: among 151 with advanced disease, immune checkpoint therapy (alone or with chemotherapy) gave higher response rates and longer survival than chemotherapy alone; with PD-L1 ≥50%, median real-world overall survival was 30 months (John et al., *Clin Cancer Res* 2026, DOI 10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-25-2581). *Bearing:* supports immunotherapy at recurrence; retrospective.
- **Adjuvant immunotherapy is not uniformly beneficial.** The BR.31 phase 3 trial of adjuvant durvalumab after resection found no disease-free survival benefit, regardless of PD-L1 (Goss et al., *J Clin Oncol* 2026, DOI 10.1200/JCO-25-01828). *Bearing:* durvalumab is not labeled for this use; the result is context for weighing adjuvant immunotherapy. A September 2026 perspective reports that the phase 3 EA5142/ALCHEMIST trial of adjuvant nivolumab after standard postoperative treatment showed no disease-free survival benefit in EGFR/ALK-negative resected NSCLC (Yazgan et al., *Front Oncol* 2026, DOI 10.3389/fonc.2026.1941683; secondary report, primary results not reviewed).
- **ctDNA after surgery.** A meta-analysis of 13 studies (1,309 patients) found that detectable ctDNA after surgery or after adjuvant chemotherapy predicted recurrence (e.g., post-chemotherapy positivity, hazard ratio 2.85) (Khan et al., *Clin Transl Oncol* 2026, DOI 10.1007/s12094-025-04182-8). *Bearing:* prognostic only; no labeled treatment is selected by ctDNA.
- **TMB in early-stage lung cancer.** In a genomic-profiling cohort, TMB ≥10 mut/Mb was associated with longer time to recurrence with adjuvant immune checkpoint inhibitors (Quintanilha et al., *J Immunother Cancer* 2026, DOI 10.1136/jitc-2025-014168). *Bearing:* exploratory; consistent with, but not proof of, benefit from adjuvant immunotherapy given her TMB of 31.7.

The web searches for FDA approval announcements (through mid-September 2026 in the results returned) found no approval for KRAS non-G12C lung cancer or for adjuvant treatment; later announcements may have been missed.
