Two signals drive the tumor's growth pathway at once. The NTRK3 fusion switches on a growth-signal receptor (TRK) permanently, and KRAS G12D switches on the relay switch just below it. TRK inhibitors block the receptor, but not KRAS, and reports of resistance to TRK inhibitors describe genomic changes that reactivate this pathway below the receptor (Cocco 2019, Nat Med); the abstract describes these as changes acquired during treatment, so how a KRAS mutation present from the start affects response isn't known. The fusion was found only on the report, its breakpoints aren't given, and one recorded VPS18-NTRK3 form was non-functional, so it's worth confirming that the fusion is expressed (RNA testing or pan-TRK staining). SMAD4, a brake in the TGF-beta pathway, has lost one of its two copies in a focal deletion (next to a broader chromosome 18 loss); one copy remains and no second hit was found, so whether the brake is fully off is worth confirming. TET2 E576K is of uncertain significance and isn't counted. The other drivers are broad low-level gains of little known significance. Because variant allele fractions differ and tumor purity isn't recorded, whether the KRAS mutation and the fusion are in the same cells (clonal) can't be determined. ## Information gaps

- **Whether the NTRK3 fusion is functional and expressed.** The fusion is on the report only, without breakpoints, and the larotrectinib and entrectinib labels select patients with an FDA-approved test (repotrectinib's indication text doesn't state a test requirement). An RNA-based fusion test or pan-TRK immunohistochemistry on tumor tissue would confirm it; TRIDENT-1 entry needs central laboratory confirmation (retrospective after a local NGS or qPCR result, prospective after FISH).
- **NTRK3 point mutations were not tested.** The test's studied regions don't include NTRK3, so resistance mutations in the TRK kinase domain can't be ruled out (the larotrectinib and entrectinib labels require no known acquired resistance mutation; he has had no TRK inhibitor).
- **MSI/MMR status.** MSI was indeterminate and no MMR immunohistochemistry is recorded. MSI-H/dMMR would make pembrolizumab, nivolumab with ipilimumab and dostarlimab labeled options, and the pelareorep trial requires a non-MSI-H/pMMR result. NTRK fusions in colorectal cancer are described mainly in MSI-H tumors (see Genomic findings). MLH1 promoter methylation can't be assessed from these data.
- **Germline status.** The test reports acquired findings only; no germline testing or family history is recorded.
- **Response to prior regimens, neuropathy and measurable disease.** The record doesn't give the best response to either oxaliplatin regimen (the VS-7375 trial requires at least stable disease on a prior therapy), the grade of any oxaliplatin neuropathy after 24 cycles (the repotrectinib trial excludes neuropathy of grade 2 or higher, and the pelareorep trial ongoing treatment-related adverse events of grade 2 or higher), or measurable lesions by RECIST (required by most trials). No current CEA.
- **Regions not studied:** NTRK3 and KDR (both on the report's panel), NRG1, VEGFA, HLA-C, PPP1R15A and SLFN11 have no studied region, so variants there are not tested. Fusions and other rearrangements aren't among the test's variant types in the genomic data.
- **SMAD4 second copy.** No small variant was found in SMAD4's studied regions; other ways of losing the remaining copy (e.g. methylation) aren't measured.

His last progression (2026-07-21) came about 9 months after his last oxaliplatin dose, later than those trials required. ECOG 0 and normal labs.

**2. Larotrectinib (Vitrakvi), or entrectinib (Rozlytrek) or repotrectinib (Augtyro), for the NTRK3 fusion**

CIViC level A in solid tumors (EID6568, EID11125).

Larotrectinib has traditional approval; entrectinib and repotrectinib are accelerated approvals with less colorectal data, so larotrectinib is preferred among the three. Oral; ECOG 0 and normal organ function.

Confirming that the fusion is expressed would strengthen the case for using it, either now or after FOLFIRI-based therapy; it remains labeled at every later line.

## Emerging evidence

Searches covered 2024-06-13 to 2026-11-18 (PubMed, FDA announcements via web search, meeting abstracts as indexed).

- **Larotrectinib in TRK fusion gastrointestinal cancers** (Eur J Cancer 2025, doi:10.1016/j.ejca.2025.115338; single-arm NAVIGATE trial): in 26 colorectal cancers (62% MSI-H), response rate 44%, median duration of response 27 months, median PFS 7 months and median OS 29 months. Supports the TRK inhibitor option; most of these tumors were MSI-H, while his MSI status is indeterminate.
- **Larotrectinib vs real-world standard care** (JCO Precis Oncol 2025, doi:10.1200/PO-24-00500; matched comparison across five tumor types including colorectal): longer OS with larotrectinib (HR 0.44). Non-randomized.
- **REALTRK registry** (JCO Precis Oncol 2026, doi:10.1200/PO-26-00283; 47 adults, colorectal among the commonest): response rate 46% to a TRK inhibitor given as first treatment after fusion diagnosis. Real-world, not specific to colorectal cancer.
- **GFH375, oral KRAS G12D inhibitor** (Nat Med 2026, doi:10.1038/s41591-026-04559-4; GFH375 is the same drug as VS-7375 per Verastem Oncology's pipeline and announcements, retrieved 2026-11-18; phase 1, 74 patients including 10 with colorectal cancer): responses reported in pancreatic and lung cancers (about 35%); the abstract gives no colorectal response rate. Bears on the VS-7375 trials above.
- **Zanzalintinib plus atezolizumab** for previously treated metastatic colorectal cancer (STELLAR-303, randomized phase 3 vs regorafenib, 901 patients: median OS 10.9 vs 9.4 months, HR 0.80, in the intention-to-treat population; OncLive, 2026-09-11): the FDA accepted the application (Targeted Oncology, 2026-02-02) and extended the action date from 2026-12-03 to 2027-03-03 (OncLive, 2026-09-11). Not approved as of the search; if approved, it would be a later-line option whose label would need checking against his MSI status.
- **Daraxonrasib (Rasonque)** was approved 2026-08 for previously treated metastatic pancreatic cancer only (label 2026-08-27); no colorectal indication.
