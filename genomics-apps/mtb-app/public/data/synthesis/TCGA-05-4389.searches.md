## 8. Fact-check corrections (2026-11-17)

Lookups made to verify the corrections from an independent fact-check:
- PubMed efetch PMID 32182156 (lifirafenib): confirmed responses in BRAF V600 melanoma, thyroid and LGSOC; one BRAF-mutated NSCLC had an unconfirmed PR (variant not given).
- PubMed efetch PMID 39293516 (AMG 193): eligibility was CDKN2A-deleted and/or MTAP-deleted tumors; CIViC EID12158 notes 6 MTAP-intact (central IHC) patients excluded from the efficacy analysis.
- BR.31 abstract (PMID 41529222) says "randomly assigned"; no phase given, so it is now described as randomized.
- Web search for "taletrectinib Ibtrozi FDA approval June 2025 ROS1": first approved 2025-06-11 (FDA "FDA approves taletrectinib for ROS1-positive NSCLC"); 2026-09-17 was a supplemental approval.
- ClinicalTrials.gov records re-read: NCT02693535 has 174 US sites (146 recruiting, 28 active not recruiting); NCT07513376 inclusion criteria (above); NCT04913285 exclusion "Known participants who have received local therapy with either surgery and/or radiation therapy (participants with asymptomatic untreated brain metastasis may be eligible…)" and Part B exclusion of prior BRAF/MEK/MAPK inhibitors; NCT05786924 key inclusion does not require measurable disease.
- Labels re-read: Tecentriq first-line monotherapy is for metastatic NSCLC with TC ≥50% or IC ≥10% by an FDA-authorized test; Keytruda single agent covers stage III not suitable for resection or definitive chemoradiation, or metastatic; Libtayo covers locally advanced not suitable for resection or chemoradiation, or metastatic, TPS ≥50% by an FDA-approved test. Braftovi's limitation of use is for wild-type BRAF.
- CIViC items added: EID7444 (accepted, B, NSCLC, CDKN2A Loss, palbociclib; same TAPUR cohort) and EID7407 (submitted, B, lung squamous, CCND3 amplification, palbociclib, does not support).
