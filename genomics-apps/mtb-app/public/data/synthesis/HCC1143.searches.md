## 2. Genes searched and why

- **Minimum (round 1):** reported genes TP53, FGFR2. Always-search (tumor-agnostic) list: NTRK1–3, BRAF, RET, MLH1, MSH2, MSH6, PMS2, ERBB2. Searched as batches "min1" and "min2" (24 genes).
- **Driver sweep (round 1):** all 858 ONCOGENE / TSG / ONCOGENE_AND_TSG genes in `OncoKB_CancerGeneList.tsv` (downloaded 2026-11-06), which include all minimum genes, in batches of 20 (b00–b41), GRCh38 gene ranges, each batch: one `$find-subject-variants`, one `$find-subject-structural-intersecting-variants`, and `$find-study-metadata` with 3 ranges per request.
- **Round 2 (questions raised by round 1 and the evidence):** failed-liftover genes (CRLF2, NCOA4, PDE4DIP) split and retried; the six genes with no server coordinates; TP53 re-queried with its GRCh37 range and a widened GRCh38 range (server anomaly check); UGT1A1 (Trodelvy label warning on UGT1A1*28); phenotype operations for TP53, FGFR2, HDAC4, NUP98 variants. Evidence-driven questions also answered in round 2 without new genome queries: FGFR2 "activating mutation" (trial NCT06302621) → literature; PTEN loss (trials NCT04997993, NCT07109726) → PTEN deletion already found; MAGI2/HDAC4 evidence and trials → none.
- **Round 3:** the round-2 findings raised no further genomic question (no evidence or trial named a gene or variant not yet searched). Loop stopped.

Server etiquette: one request at a time, 2 s pause after each, back-off and split on 503 (one 503 seen, batch b10 structural search, split and completed). Coordinator method note received mid-run: metadata ranges should be one gene per request, up to 3 when the server is quiet; 3 were used throughout (server response 6–7 s, no metadata errors except liftover 422s).

### Server anomalies found (results corrected before interpretation)
1. **Matches outside the queried range.** The variant searches return records whose coordinates fall outside the gene in the record's own build, apparently because the query range and its lifted-over range are both compared against data from both builds. Examples: TP53 structural search returned a deletion recorded as NC_000017.10:7668409-7668440 (GRCh37; outside TP53, whose GRCh37 range is 7571738-7590808; re-querying with the GRCh37 TP53 range returned it again). *Corrected 2026-11-23: that record was a GRCh38 position mislabeled GRCh37; the data now hold it at its GRCh37 position, NC_000017.10:7571737-7571748 (outer bounds 7571727-7571758), inside TP53's 3′ UTR. Not a driver; noted in the synthesis.* NF1 returned NC_000017.11:29609162:G:C (GRCh38 position inside NF1's GRCh37 range, not its GRCh38 range). In 17 simple-variant and 8 structural cases the record didn't overlap the gene in its own build; these were excluded (listed below).
2. **Chromosome ignored in one case.** The CAMTA1 search (chr1:6785451-7769706) returned NC_000017.11:7668419:GTGGCAGCAAAGT:GT (chr17, TP53 3′ UTR by VEP), but the TP53 search and a widened TP53 search (NC_000017.11:7668400-7687550) did not. This variant was not used. *Corrected 2026-11-23: the record was filed under chr1 by mistake and duplicated the TP53 3′ UTR deletion above; it has been removed from the data.*
3. **Female patient with Y-chromosome structural calls:** 10 distinct structural events on NC_000024.9 (spanning KDM5D). Raises a question about the structural data's provenance; noted under Information gaps.
4. **All small variants have VAF 0.25** (placeholder): clonality can't be assessed.
